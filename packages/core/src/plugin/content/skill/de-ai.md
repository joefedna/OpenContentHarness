# De-AI checklist

Readers spot machine-written text quickly and trust it less. Find and fix these tells. The goal is writing that is specific and has a point of view, not writing that merely avoids banned words.

## Words and phrases to cut or replace
delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape (figurative), navigate (figurative), embark, journey (for anything that is not travel), unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, leverage (as a verb), harness, foster, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize, transformative, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, myriad, plethora, multifaceted, ever-evolving, in today's fast-paced world, in the digital age, it's worth noting that, it's important to note, at the end of the day, when it comes to, in conclusion, moreover, furthermore, additionally (at sentence start), dive in, let's dive deep, buckle up, here's the kicker, the secret sauce, a deep dive, stands as, serves as, boasts.

## Structures to break up
- "It's not X, it's Y" and "This isn't just X. It's Y." used more than once per piece.
- Groups of three adjectives or three parallel clauses everywhere. Vary the count.
- Every paragraph the same length; every sentence the same rhythm. Mix short and long.
- Rhetorical questions answered right away ("The result? A 40% lift.") used as a habit.
- Em dashes everywhere. Use commas, periods or parentheses instead in most places.
- Openers that restate the prompt, and closers that summarize what was just said or wish the reader luck.
- Lists where prose would read better, and bold text scattered through paragraphs.
- Hedging stacks: "may potentially help to somewhat improve".
- Emoji used as bullet points, unless the brand does it on purpose.
- Fake balance: "While X has benefits, it also has drawbacks" without saying which matters more.

## What to add instead
- A real specific: a number, a name, a date, a place, a quote, a screenshot description.
- An opinion: say which option is better and why.
- A moment: a short scene or example from the brand's own experience.
- The brand's actual vocabulary, including informal words and contractions if it uses them.
- A sentence that only this brand could have written.
