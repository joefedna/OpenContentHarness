# Platform formats

Check the brand profile for platform-specific rules first; these are defaults. Platform limits change, so verify with web search when a limit matters.

## LinkedIn post
- Hook within about 210 characters, before the "see more" cut. Short lines and white space. 800 to 1,500 characters performs well; the limit is 3,000.
- Put links in the first comment rather than the post when reach matters. Three hashtags at most, or none.
- End with a question or a clear point of view to draw comments.
- Carousels (PDF documents): 6 to 12 slides, one idea per slide, large text, a hook slide and a CTA slide.

## X (Twitter)
- 280 characters per post unless the account has long posts. Threads of 6 to 12 posts, each standing alone. No links in the first post when reach matters.

## Instagram
- Carousel: up to 20 slides, hook on slide 1, save-worthy value, CTA on the last slide. Captions up to 2,200 characters with the hook in the first 125.
- Reels: hook in the first second, captions on screen, 15 to 90 seconds.

## TikTok and short video (Reels, Shorts)
- Spoken and visual hook in the first one to two seconds. One idea per video. On-screen text for silent viewing. Pattern interrupts (cut, angle change, B-roll) every three to five seconds.
- Script format: HOOK, then numbered beats with VISUAL and VO lines, then CTA. 30 to 60 seconds is about 75 to 150 spoken words.

## YouTube long-form
- Title under about 60 characters plus a thumbnail concept with three words or fewer of text. Promise in the title, payoff confirmed in the first 30 seconds.
- Script: cold open, why to keep watching, chapters with an open loop at each transition, recap and CTA. Write chapters with timestamps for the description.

## Newsletter and email
- Subject under about 50 characters, preview text that adds to the subject rather than repeating it. One main idea and one primary CTA. Plain-looking emails often outperform designed ones for personal brands.
- Sequences: welcome (deliver the promise, tell the story, set expectations), nurture, launch (announce, proof, objections, deadline), re-engagement.

## Blog and SEO article
- Match search intent. Title tag under 60 characters, meta description under 155. Headers that answer sub-questions. Original examples or data that the ranking pages lack.

## Ads
- Meta: primary text with the hook first, headline up to about 40 characters, description up to about 30. Write three to five variants that differ on one variable each.
- Google search: headlines of 30 characters (up to 15), descriptions of 90 (up to 4). Include the keyword and a specific benefit.

## Podcast
- Episode title with a clear outcome, show notes with timestamps and links, and three to five clips for short video.
